( 01 )

Positioning

A boutique hotel feeling at home. Not a cleaning company — a premium home care ritual.

SUKODA is not a cleaning company. It is a premium home care ritual: always the same person who knows the home, fresh flowers, seasonal fruit and a handwritten welcome card at every visit.

The positioning is deliberately in the highest price class, where very few companies in Estonia operate with a comparable concept.

The brand is bilingual ET/EN, the first target market is Tallinn's premium residential areas, and the concept can be extended to other cities and countries.

( 02 )

Service Standard

Tested, not just designed.

The SUKODA service has been delivered to a pilot client for six months and 12 visits. The process has been tested from start to finish.

Every visit includes fresh flowers from Fleur Royale, with whom a partnership agreement is in place. Added to this are exclusive chocolate and, as a finishing touch, the home is scented with an Italian artisan perfume, carefully selected from several candidates.

The service is carried by a highly experienced lead housekeeper whose touch defines SUKODA's level of detail: towels are folded into fans and boutique hotel finishing techniques are used throughout. She has agreed to continue as a trainer and quality controller for new staff.

The service standard is documented in writing and does not depend on one person. The buyer does not need to invent it — it exists and can be handed over.

From a pilot visit: flowers, seasonal fruit, exclusive chocolate and a welcome card on the client's table.

Business Model

Subscriptions and automatic billing.

1× per month €199 /month
2× per month €279 /month
4× per month €499 /month

Prices are set and published on the site, but they can be changed freely: during the onboarding month the seller reconfigures prices, packages and copy exactly to the buyer's business logic.

The subscription model gives predictable cash flow from the first client. Gift cards bring additional revenue and work especially well as corporate gifts and as a closing gift in real estate transactions.

For growth, a platform model has also been worked out, where contracted partners deliver the service and SUKODA keeps a commission — expansion without a large payroll.
